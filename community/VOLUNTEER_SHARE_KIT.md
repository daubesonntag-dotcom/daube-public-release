# D’AUBE Volunteer Share Kit

Use these short calls when a community explicitly allows feedback, beta-test, show-and-tell, open-source contribution, or volunteer-compute posts.

**Do not spam individuals or unrelated projects. Do not ask for a positive review, rating, reciprocal review, purchase, signup, private-system access, or secret material.** Keep every external report tied to the exact evidence class it proves.

Canonical intake:

- Viewer / UX / browser feedback: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/176
- Browser CPU / WebGPU benchmark: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/87
- Opt-in Compute Commons host: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/96
- Future physical packaging / unboxing interest: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/177

## 1 — Five-minute viewer call

> I’m looking for one brutally simple piece of feedback on an unusual public web experience. No signup, purchase, positive review, or test-for-test exchange.
>
> Spend 30 seconds on D’AUBE Work Arcade, click the first thing you naturally want to click, and stop at the first moment you hesitate, feel lost, distrust something, or almost close the page.
>
> Report that exact moment + device/browser here: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/176
>
> Five honest minutes are enough. Negative feedback is useful.

Best fit: beta-test / side-project / indie-builder / UX feedback communities that explicitly invite app links.

## One-flow rule

When a community asks what to test, name **one flow only**. For the current Viewer campaign, use:

`30-second comprehension → first natural click → first hesitation`

Do not ask a stranger to “test the whole ecosystem.” A precise five-minute observation is more useful than a vague full-site review.

## Evidence classification

| External action | What it may prove | What it never proves by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent viewer report | usability / comprehension / browser behavior observed | customer, payment, revenue |
| Browser benchmark receipt | measured public-safe browser execution on that session | persistent capacity, SLA, private-runtime suitability |
| Host opt-in comment | willingness / source provenance | measured GPU/CPU capacity or production readiness |
| Measured admitted host canary | bounded runtime capability in the measured scope | commercial SLA or customer delivery |
| Physical-pilot interest comment | independent interest signal | order, production, shipment, acceptance, revenue |

## Community etiquette

- Read each community’s current rules before posting.
- Prefer recurring feedback/beta-test/showcase threads over unrelated posts.
- Never mass-DM people who did not invite project links.
- Do not offer payment unless a separately funded Mission exists with an explicit amount and terms.
- Do not condition acknowledgement, future access, or rewards on positive feedback.
- Do not count Founder-owned, bot, synthetic, or related-party activity as independent evidence.
- Keep passwords, tokens, IDs, banking data, private customer data, private URLs, and exploit details out of public issues.
