# D’AUBE Mission Board

## Paid Missions

**No funded paid Mission is currently represented by this file as claimable.**

A paid Mission appears here only after D’AUBE verifies:
- bounded scope and deliverables;
- objective acceptance criteria;
- engagement classification;
- maximum payout;
- funding/payment basis;
- cancellation/partial-acceptance rule;
- access/security requirements;
- acceptance owner.

Do not start paid production work based on an idea, discussion, draft issue or future promise that is not explicitly marked funded/authorized.

## Interest Pool

You may join the Talent Pool before paid Missions exist. This helps D’AUBE match future work without pretending current vacancies or funding exist.

## Commons

Genuinely optional public/open-source/community contributions, when published, are listed separately and are never described as paid work unless a funded reward is explicitly attached.
