Commercial clarity

What the price does — and does not — mean.

Price is only one part of the transaction. Delivery, taxes, currency, support and licensing stay visible as separate terms.

Taxes & currency

The final checkout amount may include applicable sales tax or VAT based on buyer location. The buyer sees the payable amount before confirming payment.

Delivery

Eligible digital products are delivered electronically after successful payment by download, access link, account entitlement or another method stated on the product page.

Subscriptions

D’AUBE will not charge recurring fees unless checkout clearly identifies a subscription, billing interval, renewal terms and cancellation method.

Bespoke services

Human services are handled separately from eligible digital-product checkout and are only confirmed after scope review.

Customer support

Questions about products, delivery or licensing: hello@daubesonntag.com. Order-specific refund and buyer-support routes remain available through the relevant checkout provider when applicable.
