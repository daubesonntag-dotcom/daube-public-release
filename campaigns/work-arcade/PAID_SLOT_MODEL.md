# D’AUBE Work Arcade — Paid Slot Model

> **Rent the work. Pay for the experience. Keep the proof.**

D’AUBE Work Arcade is a Reverse Work Economy: participants pay to enter a bounded, designed work experience rather than being hired into an employment relationship.

## Two lanes — never blur them

### Lane A — Paid Work Experience
The participant is the customer. The fee buys a defined service package: mission design, sandbox access, D’AUBE tools/operator support, review, evidence packaging, progression and optional public showcase.

The participant is not buying a job, salary, employment, internship, production admission, investment return or guaranteed commercial outcome.

### Lane B — Production Contribution
If D’AUBE later wants to use a participant artifact in production or monetize it, that use requires a separate contribution/licensing agreement. Compensation, revenue share, credits or another negotiated benefit must be explicit. The paid experience fee does not silently transfer commercial rights to D’AUBE.

## What the customer owns

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the participant retains rights to their original contribution and may use their exported artifact/evidence in a portfolio, subject to third-party rights and confidential-information restrictions.

## What D’AUBE may use by default

D’AUBE may use operational metadata needed to run the experience and, only with the participant’s permission, non-confidential screenshots, excerpts or showcase material. Commercial production use requires a separate agreement.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

- no fake credential, academic credit, job guarantee or employment promise
- no hidden production labor
- no forced positive review
- no pay-to-win production admission
- no unrestricted production credentials
- no government-ID, password, OTP, card or secret data in public issues
- no commercial use of participant work without an explicit rights basis
- refund/support/privacy/terms must be visible before payment

## Commercial activation gate

Real checkout stays OFF until D’AUBE has direct evidence for:
1. public offer description + final price
2. terms and consumer disclosures
3. refund/cancellation policy
4. privacy/data handling
5. IP/license terms
6. payment/settlement path
7. entitlement and experience delivery
8. support + complaint handling
9. production-contribution agreement path
10. accounting/tax treatment

Until then, the public flow captures **paid-slot interest**, not payment.
