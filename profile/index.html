Professional development

Continuous learning.
No decorative credentials.

D’AUBE uses an evidence-gated continuing professional development model: official learning sources, internal study artifacts, independent evaluation, recency tracking and issuer verification before any external credential is published.

Professional methods

Industry structure,
without borrowed prestige.

The competency model is informed by SFIA 9 concepts of skills and responsibility.
