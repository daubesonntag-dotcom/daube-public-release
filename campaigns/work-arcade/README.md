# D’AUBE Work Arcade — Public Beta

> **Rent the work. Build the world. Leave a mark.**

What if a company sold you the *job* instead of hiring you for it?

D’AUBE Work Arcade is a public experimental **Reverse Work Simulation** where builders can choose unusual work experiences, set their own KPI and deadline, build inside one of **36 evolving Worlds**, submit evidence, earn Proof Capsules and titles, explore World Forge, and inspect the trust/economy rules behind the experience.

## Important beta truth

This is currently a **public browser simulation plus paid-slot validation catalog**.

- Real charge: **OFF until commerce admission passes**
- Real cash payout: **OFF until separate contribution/payout terms pass**
- Production admission cannot be purchased
- Commercial participation requires separate operative terms
- External community compute is not claimed until independently evidenced

The paid-slot catalog uses explicit validation prices, but the current public flow captures interest rather than payment. D’AUBE will not enable checkout until consumer disclosures, refund/cancellation, privacy, IP, payment, delivery, support, accounting/tax and contribution-rights gates have direct evidence.

## Who should try it?

### Developers / engineers
Rent a bounded engineering problem, define your own benchmark, submit reproducible evidence, or try a restoration/boss challenge.

### Game developers / worldbuilders
Pick a World Plot, propose terrain, architecture, flora, fauna, weather, sound, portals, interactions or bounded World Laws. Canon admission remains evidence-gated.

### Designers / artists / sound creators
Build visual, interaction, atmospheric or audio systems that can become part of a governed World genealogy instead of disappearing in a one-off portfolio post.

### AI / compute people
Inspect the Compute Commons model: explicit host consent, resource caps, work units, independent result verification and post-verification rewards.

### Curious people
Rent the Boss, rent a Mystery, or enter a strange mission just to see whether a job can feel more like a playable world than a vacancy listing.

## Why would anyone pay to rent work?

The intended service is not “pay us so you can perform unpaid employment.” The product is the **experience, infrastructure, sandbox, challenge design, tools, compute access, evidence system, progression and potential commercial pathway**—similar in spirit to a workshop, simulation, game, residency, hackathon or professional challenge, but organized as a persistent playable economy.

The commercial model will only turn on after the applicable legal, accounting, consumer, IP, payment and payout gates are admitted.
