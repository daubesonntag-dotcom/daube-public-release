# D’AUBE SONNTAG — Talent Pool

D’AUBE is building an evidence-based Talent Pool for future paid missions, contract work, employment opportunities and genuinely optional Commons contributions.

## Important status

**This is an interest/talent pool, not a promise of employment or paid work.**

D’AUBE currently uses a fail-closed funding rule: a paid mission is not advertised as executable until its scope, acceptance criteria, payout cap and funding/payment basis are verified internally. We do not ask people to perform production work for free while waiting for funding.

## Who can join

People with useful skills in areas such as:
- design, UI/UX and visual systems;
- software, web, data, AI/ML and automation;
- QA, accessibility, security and technical writing;
- video, audio, illustration, 3D and creative production;
- research, localization and content;
- operations, customer experience, finance/admin support and studio workflows.

You do not need to fit a traditional job title. D’AUBE matches people to bounded Missions using skills, portfolio evidence, reliability, availability and mission-specific requirements.

## How the future Mission system works

`funded demand -> published Mission -> self-claim/apply -> match/review -> authorization -> delivery -> acceptance -> payout/reputation`

Low-risk missions are designed to become self-service so the Founder does not need to manually assign every task. High-risk legal, financial, security, ownership and public-regulated actions keep stricter review gates.

## Join the Talent Pool

Open a **Talent Pool** issue in this repository and share only information you are comfortable making public:
- display name / handle;
- skills;
- portfolio or public work links;
- preferred work type;
- availability;
- approximate rate/bounty preference if you want;
- languages/time zone if relevant.

Do **not** post government ID, bank information, home address, passwords, API keys, private customer data or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Joining the pool is free. There is no application fee and no pay-to-rank mechanism.

## Candidate tests

Default tests/auditions are portfolio review, synthetic exercises or non-production tasks. If D’AUBE wants to rely on usable production work, the work must move into an appropriately paid/contracted lane before D’AUBE relies on it.

## Commons contribution

Optional open-source/community contributions may be offered separately. They must be genuinely voluntary and must not substitute for required paid production work or customer deliverables.
