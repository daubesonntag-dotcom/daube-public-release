PUBLIC TRUST · MONEY FIRST

Proof before promotion.

D’AUBE separates what is demonstrated, implemented, deployed, delivered to a customer and verified as settled revenue. A stronger label requires stronger evidence; a merge, checkout, invoice, canary or screenshot cannot promote itself.

01

DEMO

Prototype or demonstration only. No production, customer or revenue implication.

02

IMPLEMENTED

Traceable code or configuration exists. Deployment is not implied.

03

DEPLOYED · HEALTHY

Runtime/environment and time-bounded health readback are required.

04

CUSTOMER DELIVERY

Commercial basis plus delivery/acceptance evidence, with privacy controls.

05

REVENUE VERIFIED

Externally settled and reconciled customer cash; tests and related-party canaries do not qualify.

RIGHTS & ATTRIBUTION

Evidence must also be publishable.

Allowed rights bases

  • D’AUBE original
  • Commissioned with commercial rights
  • Licensed for the stated use
  • Open-license or public-domain basis
  • Customer-owned for that customer’s delivery only

Never inferred

  • Community endorsement or traditional authority
  • Geographic/cultural authenticity without evidence
  • Redistribution rights from mere access
  • Permission to publish customer-confidential material
  • Revenue proof from checkout or test-payment evidence

Public proof is a disclosure layer over canonical evidence. It never becomes the source of truth for order, payment, settlement, customer identity, rights ownership or deployment health.
