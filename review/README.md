## Review rubric

Score each direction from 1–5 on:

- D’AUBE distinctiveness / ownability
- Emotional impact in first 5 seconds
- Brand meaning clarity
- Premium / cultural authority
- Hero art direction
- Four Doors discoverability
- Content hierarchy / readability
- Mobile composition
- Accessibility and focus clarity
- Motion potential without motion dependence
- Extensibility to Studio, Ailleurs, Veritas, Hospitality and future business lines
- Performance feasibility

Recommended shortlist rule: keep the top 3 by total score, then select a winner only after testing real production assets and mobile widths.
