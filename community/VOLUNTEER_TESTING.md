# BREAK THE DAWN — D’AUBE Volunteer Tester & Viewer Program

D’AUBE SONNTAG is inviting independent people to **look, click, question, confuse, break, and critique** its public surfaces before wider release.

The goal is not praise. The useful signal is the moment you hesitate, misunderstand, almost leave, hit a broken path, or think “why is this here?”

## Who can participate

You do not need to be a developer.

Useful perspectives include:

- first-time viewers who can give a 5-minute first impression;
- Android / iPhone / tablet users;
- Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and in-app-browser users;
- keyboard-only, zoom, reduced-motion or screen-reader users;
- designers, developers and QA testers;
- small-business owners, creators, freelancers and potential D’AUBE users;
- people who simply enjoy trying new web experiences and giving precise feedback.

## Choose a test lane

### Lane A — 5-minute Viewer

Open a public D’AUBE surface and answer:

1. What do you think D’AUBE is within the first 30 seconds?
2. What is the first action you expect to take?
3. Where did you hesitate or almost close the page?
4. What feels trustworthy? What feels unclear?
5. What single change would improve the experience most?

### Lane B — Mobile Friction Hunt

Spend about 10–15 minutes on a phone or tablet.

Look for:

- clipped or overlapping content;
- buttons that are hard to tap;
- keyboard/form issues;
- unexpected horizontal scrolling;
- in-app browser problems;
- slow or confusing transitions;
- unclear error/recovery states;
- anything that makes you want to abandon the flow.

### Lane C — Accessibility Pass

Public-surface observations are welcome for:

- keyboard navigation and focus visibility;
- semantic labels and screen-reader clarity;
- 200%+ zoom and text resizing;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- contrast/readability;
- form instructions and error recovery.

Do not publish exploit details or sensitive security material in a normal issue.

### Lane D — Commerce Journey Observer

You may inspect a **public** browse/order journey and report confusion around products, pricing, policies, consent, trust, or fulfillment expectations.

**No purchase is required. Stop before any real payment, contractual commitment, shipping submission, or other action you do not genuinely want to perform.**

A volunteer test is not a customer order, payment, settlement, acceptance, shipment or revenue record.

### Lane E — Technical Public-Surface Tester

For developers/QA:

- reproduce public bugs;
- inspect ordinary browser behavior;
- test public responsive states;
- review public repository documentation and openly published interfaces;
- submit small public/open fixes only where the contribution scope allows it.

Do not target private infrastructure, bypass access controls, collect third-party data, or publish secrets/exploit payloads.

## What a strong report contains

Please include as much of this as practical:

- **surface / URL or repo path**;
- **device + OS**;
- **browser + version**;
- **test lane**;
- **what you tried**;
- **what you expected**;
- **what actually happened**;
- **the exact moment you became confused or nearly left**;
- **severity**: cosmetic / friction / blocker;
- optional screenshot or recording containing **public-safe data only**.

A short precise report is more useful than a long compliment.

## What participants get

Participation is voluntary and currently unpaid unless a separate funded Mission explicitly says otherwise.

When appropriate, participants may receive:

- optional public acknowledgement as a Founding Tester / Viewer;
- priority invitation to future public pilots;
- visibility into fixes that resulted from their report;
- the option to join the separate Talent Pool for possible future paid Missions.

None of these is a promise of employment, paid work, compensation, product entitlement or commercial relationship.

## Feedback ethics

D’AUBE does **not** require a positive rating or positive review.

Please be candid. A negative or critical observation is useful when it is specific and reproducible.

Never post:

- government ID or banking details;
- passwords, API keys, private keys or recovery codes;
- private customer information;
- unpublished internal D’AUBE material;
- data you do not have the right to share.

## Evidence boundary

A genuine independent volunteer report may count as **external usability / viewer evidence** for the specific public behavior observed.

It does **not** become evidence of:

- a paying customer;
- authoritative settlement;
- physical production;
- carrier acceptance or delivery;
- customer commercial acceptance;
- revenue;
- a positive review obligation.

Those gates remain separate and require their own real-world evidence.

## How to join

Open the **Volunteer Tester / Viewer report** issue form in this repository, choose a lane, and submit one bounded observation.

Start small. Five honest minutes are useful.

**D’AUBE wants the crack before the sunrise — not the compliment after launch.**
