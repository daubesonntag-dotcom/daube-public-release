# Hire D’AUBE — Automation Sprint

Bring **one real, bounded workflow problem**. D’AUBE will first check fit, scope, required inputs, acceptance evidence and material security/privacy/rights constraints. A fit review is not an order and does not require posting secrets or private customer data.

## Best fit

- repetitive manual handoff;
- small integration or automation bottleneck;
- recurring reporting/data-transfer task;
- digital-operations workflow that can be defined with clear inputs and outputs;
- a process that currently depends on copy/paste, repeated browser work or fragile manual steps.
