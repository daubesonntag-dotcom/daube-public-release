# Contributing to D’AUBE SONNTAG

Thank you for your interest in D’AUBE.

This public repository accepts **public-safe feedback, documentation improvements, reproducible bug reports, accessibility/UX observations, and explicitly scoped Commons contributions**. It also hosts a **Talent Pool** for people who may want future paid Missions or other separately governed work.

## Choose the right lane

### 0. Volunteer Tester / Viewer — BREAK THE DAWN

Use [`community/VOLUNTEER_TESTING.md`](community/VOLUNTEER_TESTING.md) and the **Volunteer Tester / Viewer report** issue form when you want to spend roughly 5–15 minutes giving candid feedback on a public D’AUBE surface.

Useful signals include first-impression confusion, mobile friction, accessibility observations, public commerce-journey confusion, and reproducible public bugs. No purchase, positive review or positive rating is required.

A genuine independent report may be treated as external usability/viewer evidence for the specific behavior observed. It is **not** payment, settlement, physical production, shipment, revenue, customer commercial acceptance or proof of a positive review.

### 1. Talent Pool

Use [`talent/README.md`](talent/README.md) and the **Talent Pool** issue form when you want D’AUBE to know about your skills, portfolio and availability for possible future work.

Joining the pool:
- is free;
- is not an employment offer;
- is not a promise of paid work;
- does not require production work;
- must not include sensitive identity, banking, credential or customer data.

### 2. Public feedback / Commons contribution

You may contribute voluntarily when the issue is explicitly marked as a public/community contribution and the scope is non-production or independently useful to the public project.

Examples:
- report a reproducible public-page defect;
- test keyboard/screen-reader/mobile behavior and report observations;
- improve public documentation;
- suggest clearer copy or translations;
- add a small test or fix to a clearly public/open component where the repository rights allow it.

A voluntary contribution must not be used as a disguised customer deliverable or required unpaid production labor.

### 3. Paid/commercial Mission

Do not start paid/commercial work from a general issue or a voluntary Commons issue.

D’AUBE only represents a paid Mission as executable after its scope, acceptance criteria, payout cap and funding/payment basis have been verified through the governed Talent Mesh / commercial workflow. See [`talent/MISSIONS.md`](talent/MISSIONS.md).

A public GitHub issue is not a contract, payroll record, order, payment receipt or settled-cash record.

## Before contributing

1. Read the issue and confirm the contribution lane.
2. Do not begin a large change without an accepted issue/scope.
3. Keep the change bounded and reversible.
4. Do not include secrets, private customer data, government-ID numbers, bank details, passwords, API keys, private keys or recovery codes.
5. State the source/rights basis for any third-party text, image, code, dataset or asset you introduce.
6. If AI tools materially assisted the contribution, disclose that when relevant to provenance, rights or review.
7. Do not claim production readiness, legal compliance, security assurance, revenue or customer acceptance without the corresponding evidence.

## Pull requests

A useful PR should include:
- the issue or problem it addresses;
- what changed and what did not;
- how it was tested or reviewed;
- rights/provenance notes where applicable;
- known limitations;
- rollback/revert path for material changes.

Small documentation and public-surface fixes are preferred for first contributions.

## Contributor discovery

D’AUBE uses GitHub-native contribution discovery where possible. Public issues suitable for outside help may use the standard `help wanted` or `good first issue` labels so they are easier to find through GitHub search and contribution surfaces.

## Safety and conduct

Be respectful, evidence-based and privacy-conscious. Do not post harassment, doxxing, credentials, malware, exploit payloads targeting real systems, pirated/proprietary material, or personal data that you do not have the right to share.

For a security vulnerability, do not publish exploit details or secrets in a normal public issue; use the repository’s designated security reporting path when available.

## Truth boundary

Contribution acceptance means the specific public contribution was accepted under its stated scope. It does not automatically create employment, contractor status, partnership, payment entitlement, intellectual-property ownership beyond the applicable contribution terms, production admission or a commercial relationship.
