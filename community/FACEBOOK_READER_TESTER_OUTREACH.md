## Recruitment doctrine

Ask for **honest functional feedback**, not installs, ratings, reviews, follows, reciprocal engagement or positive sentiment. A failed test is useful evidence. Never ask a volunteer to expose private Facebook content, cookies, passwords, OTPs, account IDs or session tokens.

## Current community routes verified by research

### r/alphaandbetausers
Recent August 2026 threads explicitly invite web/mobile/PWA links and independent test requests. Best fit for the desktop active-tab extension and general UX friction.

Suggested one-flow ask:

> D’AUBE is looking for one five-minute Chrome/Chromium test of a least-privilege Facebook active-tab reader. No signup, purchase, rating or test-for-test. Use only a Facebook page/post/reel you are already authorized to view. Tell us success/partial/blocker and the first confusing or privacy-concerning step. Public lab: https://d-aube-facebook-reader-lab-w75u19.v2.appdeploy.ai/ Report: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/176

### r/betatests
Recent 2026 posts include volunteers offering free website/app testing. Use the same narrow five-minute flow; do not ask for positive reviews or unrelated engagement.

### r/GooglePlayDeveloper — weekly Tester Discovery thread
The current weekly thread explicitly allows genuine testing discovery and explicitly rejects paid testing/reviews/ratings/votes/referral exchanges. Best fit for the Android installed-PWA Share-to-D’AUBE lane when asking for device/version evidence.

Suggested Android ask:

Install the public D’AUBE Reader Lab as a PWA, share a Facebook link you are authorized to view, choose D’AUBE Reader Lab, and report whether the URL reaches the local Share intake receiver. No rating/review/payment/test exchange. Do not publish private Facebook text. Lab: https://d-aube-facebook-reader-lab-w75u19.v2.appdeploy.ai/ Report: https://github.com/daubesonntag-dotcom/daube-public-release/issues/176
