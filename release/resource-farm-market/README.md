## Settlement boundary

Creating an order or rendering/scanning VietQR does not mark an order paid. Authoritative settlement requires an exact order/amount match plus provider-native or bank-native evidence. External-customer cash proof explicitly rejects synthetic, founder-originated and related-party evidence.

## Standards and physical-world boundary

Traceability templates are EPCIS-inspired but do **not** claim GS1/EPCIS certification or conformance. Digital farm/factory/restaurant/distribution orchestration does **not** prove that D’AUBE owns or operates a physical farm, livestock facility, certified food factory, restaurant or distributor network.

## Revenue truth

A catalog listing is not revenue. An order is not revenue. A payment instruction is not settlement. Farm fulfillment is not payment evidence. D’AUBE recognizes cash yield only after canonical Commerce receives and reconciles an externally funded settlement from a legitimate external customer.
