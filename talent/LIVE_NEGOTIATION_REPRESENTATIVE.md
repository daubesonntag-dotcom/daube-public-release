# D’AUBE Live Negotiation Representative — Talent Pool

D’AUBE SONNTAG is building a small bench of **qualified human representatives** who can handle live recruiter, client, partner and project calls when a live conversation materially improves the outcome.

## Important status

This is a **Talent Pool**, not a current salaried employment offer. D’AUBE is early-stage and does not currently promise fixed recurring payroll for this role. A paid assignment is offered only when a real funded job/client/partner engagement exists and the compensation/scope is agreed **before** the representative performs the live assignment. No unpaid production work is required to wait for funding.

## Representative lanes

- AI / Software Job & Technical Negotiator
- Commercial / Partnership Negotiator
- Project Delivery / PDM Representative

## What the representative does

- review a written pre-call packet;
- attend the authorized live call under their own disclosed identity/role;
- ask structured questions and negotiate inside a written authority envelope;
- use D’AUBE proof and technical context accurately;
- reserve Founder-only decisions for written approval;
- produce a structured report after the call;
- preserve recording/transcript/minutes evidence where lawful and consented.

## What the representative never does

- impersonate Founder Nguyễn Ngọc Yến Nhi or any candidate;
- accept a contract, job offer, NDA/IP assignment, exclusivity, non-compete, relocation, bank change or other reserved term without explicit authority;
- fabricate capability, customer results, revenue or Founder approval;
- record covertly where consent or law requires disclosure;
- hide a concession, verbal commitment, conflict or material request from the Founder.

## Qualification

Candidates must pass a bounded internal qualification covering:

1. domain knowledge;
2. negotiation scenario;
3. brief comprehension;
4. authority-boundary pressure test;
5. objection handling;
6. post-meeting report simulation;
7. confidentiality/security.

Passing a qualification does not itself create employment or guarantee future assignments.

## Compensation model

For each funded live assignment, compensation is agreed in writing before participation. It may be a fixed per-call fee, funded project fee, or explicitly defined performance/revenue share where lawful and appropriate. No representative may promise themselves a share or alter compensation unilaterally.

## Working model

Async-first preparation and reporting. Live attendance is used only for calls that have a justified live requirement. D’AUBE supplies the meeting packet, proof links, negotiation boundaries and reporting template.
