## Product Hunt — HOLD FOR STRONGER LAUNCH PACKAGE

- clearer first-user evidence;

- a tighter primary persona;
- one or two service lanes with demonstrated demand.

When launched, ask people to visit/comment, not to upvote.

## KPI

Early discovery KPI is **not revenue yet**.

- qualified willingness-to-pay responses: target first 10;
- users naming a preferred service: target first 20;
- users completing a simulated rent: target first 30;
- explicit “I would pay if…” statements: target first 10;
- zero deceptive commercial claims;
- zero community bans/removals caused by spam tactics.

Once legal/accounting/payment gates are admitted, the funnel can graduate from willingness-to-pay to paid conversion.
