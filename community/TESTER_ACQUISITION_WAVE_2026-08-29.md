## Wave C — GitHub-native discovery

Keep #176 labeled `help wanted` + `good first issue`. Do not open duplicate tester-count issues. Where a public D’AUBE repository already has relevant community/contributor traffic, link back to #176 rather than fragmenting evidence.

Good report classes:

- Viewer — first 30 seconds / first click / hesitation
- Mobile friction — Android, iPhone, tablet, in-app browser
- Accessibility — keyboard, focus, zoom, reduced motion, contrast/readability
- Commerce observer — understand the route and stop before any unwanted payment
- Technical public-surface QA — broken link, asset, redirect, browser/runtime issue

## Routing / triage

Every independent report is classified:

`COSMETIC | FRICTION | BLOCKER | ACCESSIBILITY | TRUST | PERFORMANCE | BROKEN_FLOW`

Then route to:

`ACKNOWLEDGE → REPRODUCE → FIX/DECLINE → VERIFY → CREDIT_REPORTER`

A reporter may receive optional public acknowledgement or future-pilot priority, but those benefits must never be conditioned on praise, ratings, public promotion, or a favorable conclusion.

## Stop conditions

Stop or change a channel when any of these occur:

- community rules prohibit the request;
- moderators ask D’AUBE to stop;
- the same audience is being repeatedly solicited;
- reports become duplicative with no new signal;
- a surface is materially broken and continued acquisition would waste testers' time;
- a channel requires payment or a new privileged OAuth/account action not separately authorized.
